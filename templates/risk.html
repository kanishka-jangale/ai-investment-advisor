Risk concepts

Volatility - how much a price swings up and down. Higher volatility means a wider range of outcomes, in both directions.

Concentration - having a large share in one asset or sector, so one bad outcome hurts more.

Liquidity - how quickly you can turn an asset into cash without a big price drop.

Potential loss - the amount you could lose in a bad period. Ask yourself whether you could wait out a loss of that size.
